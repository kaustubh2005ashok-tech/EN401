# Handout 2 (HO2): Energy Chain Data Structures & Activity Ratios

## 🎯 Scope & Objectives
Handout 2 operationalizes the Reference Energy System into quantitative relational tables:
* **InputActivityRatio**: Units of commodity consumed per unit of operational activity ($1/\text{Efficiency}$).
* **OutputActivityRatio**: Units of commodity produced per unit of activity (1.0 for single-output generators).

## 📊 Conversion Efficiencies by Technology
![Efficiency Ratios](graphs/ho2_efficiency_ratios.png)

## 🔬 Mathematical Representation
$$\text{RateOfActivity} \times \text{InputActivityRatio} = \text{Fuel Consumed}$$
$$\text{RateOfActivity} \times \text{OutputActivityRatio} = \text{Energy Delivered}$$
