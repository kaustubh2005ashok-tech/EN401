# Handout 1 (HO1): Energy Systems Modelling Concepts & Problem Setup

## 🎯 Scope & Objectives
Handout 1 establishes the conceptual foundations of energy systems planning using OSeMOSYS. It introduces the **Reference Energy System (RES)** architecture, primary vs. secondary energy carriers, conversion technologies, and unit conventions.

## 📊 Technical Units Framework
![Technical Units](graphs/ho1_units_framework.png)

* **Energy Units**: Petajoules (PJ)
* **Power / Capacity Units**: Gigawatts (GW)
* **Capital Cost Units**: Million USD per Gigawatt (M$/GW), which equals $/kW
* **Conversion Factor**: $1 \text{ GW} \cdot \text{year} = 31.536 \text{ PJ}$

## 🔬 Key Insights
1. **Reference Energy System (RES)** maps fuel extraction, transmission, and end-use demand.
2. **Deterministic Linear Programming** guarantees finding the globally optimal expansion schedule.
